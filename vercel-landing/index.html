Turn Excel sheets into charts in minutes. Upload your business data and get quick visual insights.

Open Live App Setup Guide

1) Upload Excel

Supports .xlsx/.xls. Choose the correct sheet and preview rows before charting.

2) Build Charts

Create bar, line, scatter, and histogram visualizations from numeric columns.

3) Export Clean Data

Download cleaned CSV data used in your chart for reporting and sharing.

Before deploying this frontend

  1. Deploy Streamlit app from tables/app.py to Render/Railway/Streamlit Cloud.
  2. Replace YOUR-STREAMLIT-URL.streamlit.app in index.html.
  3. Deploy this vercel-landing folder to Vercel as a static site.
